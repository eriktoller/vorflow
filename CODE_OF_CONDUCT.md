# Code of Conduct

`vorflow` follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to everyone who takes part in the project: in issues, pull
requests, reviews and any other project space, and when representing the
project elsewhere.

In short: be respectful and constructive, welcome people of every background
and level of experience, and accept that disagreement about code is about the
code. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not
accepted.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers:

- Rui Hugman — <rthugman@gmail.com>
- Oscar Sanchez — <oscarfasanchez@gmail.com>

All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. If a report concerns
one of the maintainers, contact the other.

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide on responses following the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
correction, warning, temporary ban or permanent ban, depending on the impact
of the behaviour.
